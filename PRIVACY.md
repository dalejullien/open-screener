# Privacy

Open Screener is a browser-only research tool. It has no account system and does not operate an application server.

## Data stored on your device

Settings, favourites, cached market candles, signal history, backtest results and the Forward Journal are stored in your browser using local storage and IndexedDB. Clearing site data removes them.

## Third-party requests

Your browser requests public market data directly from Binance and CoinGecko. Opening a research link can take you to TradingView, X, Binance, CoinGecko, DEX Screener, GitHub or a project's website. Those services receive ordinary web-request information and apply their own privacy policies.

Open Screener does not request exchange API keys, execute trades, use analytics cookies or sell personal data.

## Contact

For a privacy or security issue, open a GitHub issue. Do not include secrets or private account information.
